Safety Tips

1. Don't Share Personal Information

Avoid sharing your phone number, address, passwords, or other sensitive information with people you do not know.

2. Be Careful With Strangers

Be cautious if someone you do not know tries to gain your trust quickly or asks for private information.

3. Avoid Sharing Private Content

Do not send private photos or sensitive content that could be misused or cause problems later.

4. Do Not Respond to Threats or Blackmail

If someone threatens or blackmails you, do not follow their demands or send them additional content.

5. Pay Attention to Safety Alerts

If Nexa displays a safety alert about suspicious content or a conversation, stop and think carefully before continuing the interaction.

6. Use a Strong Password

Use a password that is difficult to guess and never share it with anyone.

7. Report Unsafe Behavior

If you experience threats, harassment, or suspicious behavior, use Nexa's reporting feature.

8. Trust Your Instincts

If a conversation or person makes you uncomfortable, you can end the conversation and stop interacting.
